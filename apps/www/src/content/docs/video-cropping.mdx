- **Done** keeps the crop and returns to the preview. **Remove crop** restores the full picture. Reopen the tool to edit at any time.
- Choose **Free**, an aspect-ratio preset, or **Custom** (width ÷ height). Position and size use source pixels.
- On phones, settings appear below the preview. Small selections hide side handles to keep touch targets separate; use corners or numeric fields.
- Crops support undo/redo, are saved in drafts, and apply to video, GIF, and screenshots. Captions are positioned within the cropped picture.

The native export size is labeled **Cropped**. Cropped video re-encodes even at Sharp quality. See [output sizing](/docs/export-settings#resolution) and [crop shortcuts](/docs/editor-shortcuts#crop-controls).
