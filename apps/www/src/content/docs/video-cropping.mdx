Available in Cliparr 3.1. Crop the picture to keep the region you want in video, GIF, and screenshot exports. Cropping changes the framing; the timeline still controls which part of the video plays.

## Draw and adjust a crop

1. Choose **Crop** beside the screenshot button. Playback pauses and the full picture appears with an editable selection. On desktop, you can also expand **Crop** in the **Properties** sidebar.
2. Drag across the picture to draw a rectangular crop. Drag inside an existing crop to move it, or drag a corner or side handle to resize it. To replace a crop, start drawing outside the existing selection.
3. Choose **Done** to view the cropped result. Reopen Crop whenever you want to adjust it.

The selection stays editable while the tool is active. Turning the Crop button off also keeps the crop. **Remove crop** restores the full picture and clears the aspect-ratio lock. **Center** centers the current selection without changing its size.

On a phone, use the same drawing and resizing gestures with one finger. Crop controls appear below the preview. Side handles hide when a selection is too small for separate touch targets; use the corners or numeric fields instead. Scroll outside the picture to reach the controls.

## Aspect ratio and exact values

Choose **Free** for an unrestricted rectangle, **Source** to match the video's shape, or a preset: **16:9**, **9:16**, **1:1**, **4:3**, or **4:5**. **Custom** accepts width divided by height, such as `1.5` for a 3:2 crop. A locked ratio applies while drawing or resizing.

**Position** sets X and Y from the source picture's top-left corner. **Size** sets W and H in source pixels. Press Enter or leave a field to apply it. Values stay within the picture; changing a size with a ratio locked adjusts the other dimension too. Displayed values are rounded, but simply focusing and leaving a field preserves the underlying precision.

See [Editor shortcuts](/docs/editor-shortcuts#crop-controls) for keyboard movement and temporary ratio locking with Shift.

## Save and export

Crop changes support Undo and Redo and are saved with your editor draft. A drag is one undo step. **Done** keeps the selection; it does not export a file.

Visible captions are rendered within the cropped output. Review them after choosing Done, then open Export. The native resolution option is labeled **Cropped** while a crop is active. See [Export settings](/docs/export-settings#resolution) for output sizes, pixel rounding, and re-encoding behavior.
